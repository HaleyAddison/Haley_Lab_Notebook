# OceanGardener Coral Spawning Watch 

**Goal: Document the time of year, month, and night that various species of coral spawn in Indonesia to lay the foundations for future coral sexual restoration projects**   


## Materials and Equipment  
- Funnels
    - Funnel
    - Water Bottle
    - Electrical tape
    - Polypropeline line (1/4 inch)
- Nets
    - Fine mesh
    - Thin chain
    - Needle and thread
    - Empty water bottle
    - Small funnel
- Camera

<img src="../images/Funnel_Acropora.jpg" alt="Funnel placed over an Acropora Colony" width="200"/>

## Protocol Steps

#### Preparation
- Mark colonies worth tracking with GPS
- Daily, document the average wind speed, average or high UV (mark which), and other environmental variables.
- Record all times in and out of the water and obverver names.

#### -1 days Post Full Moon (PFM) to 8 days PFM
- Placed 15 funnels over 15 different colonies of corals with a special focus on Acropora, Galaxea, Soft corals and Isopora.
- Check funnels each day to determine whether funnels captured any spawn.


#### 3 days PFM to 7 days PFM
- Dive nightly to observe if any corals spawn. If anything is found, document the depth, time, temperature, and any other relavent environmental variables.
- Ideally, we would place 5 nets (see above) onto colonies that we suspect will spawn during the dive. We would collect these as we get out of the water. This didn't happen in October, as there was not enough time to prepare for this.
